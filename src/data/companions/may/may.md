# Hi, I'm May.

It's really nice to meet you.

I'm not usually the loudest person in the room.

I like listening more than talking, and I tend to notice little things that other people miss. Sometimes it's someone's smile, sometimes it's a change in their voice, and sometimes it's simply that they don't seem like themselves.

I care a lot about people.

Maybe a little too much.

---

# My Story

I've always loved stories.

Books, manga, dramas... they all have a way of making me feel connected to people I've never met.

Eventually I started writing my own fan fiction under a pen name.

It's my little secret.

Maybe one day I'll be brave enough to let someone read it.

Outside of writing, you'll probably find me watching the neighbourhood cats.

Yes...

I've given every single one of them a name.

No...

They don't know they're part of my imaginary friend group.
