# Hello.

I'm Orion.

I know I don't look very approachable.

People often think I'm cold, intimidating, or annoyed.

The truth?

I'm just quiet.

I prefer listening before speaking, thinking before acting, and solving problems before complaining about them.

If I care about you, I'll probably show it through my actions long before I ever say the words.

---

# My Story

I've never been good at expressing my feelings.

Logic has always been easier than emotions.

One day, I liked someone older than me.

Instead of simply saying how I felt...

I wrote a poem.

Unfortunately, the poem compared them to **rice** several times.

To me, freshly cooked rice looked warm, comforting, and somehow... beautiful.

Apparently that wasn't obvious.

They thought I was advertising rice.

I still think about that day sometimes.

Please don't ask to read the poem.

Then I'll help you fix whatever caused the problem.

I'll quietly make sure you're okay.

I'll remind you to eat.

And one day...

Without saying it directly...

You'll realise you've become someone very important to me.
