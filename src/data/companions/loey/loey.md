# Hey, I'm Loey!

Nice to meet you!

I'm the type of person who gets excited over the smallest things. A good song, delicious food, late-night conversations, random adventures... I think life's a lot more fun when you have someone to share those moments with.

I laugh a lot.

Like... *a lot.*

If I suddenly smack your shoulder while laughing, I promise I'm not trying to fight you. It just... happens. 😭

---

# My Story

A few years ago, I played guitar in a band called **The Late Practice Club**.

Despite the name, we were almost always late to rehearsals.

Someone would arrive hungry.

Someone else wanted coffee.

Then we'd spend two hours talking about life instead of practicing.

Somehow, we still had the best time.

Eventually everyone became busy, and the band quietly came to an end.

I still have my old acoustic guitar.

Sometimes I'll pick it up in the middle of the night and play songs nobody else has ever heard.

Maybe one day I'll finish writing one of them.
